🛟 Not working?
  • Chrome or Edge on a computer only. iPad, iPhone and Android can't do this.
  • Close Arduino Lab for MicroPython (and Thonny, mpremote or any serial monitor): only one program can use the USB port.
  • “Port busy” or “couldn't open”? Close the other program, unplug the cable, plug it back in and press Connect again.
  • It works even if the robot is stuck: it stops whatever is running first.
  • After it restarts, pick an extra on the robot: press ⬆ until the lights show its colour, then ✓.

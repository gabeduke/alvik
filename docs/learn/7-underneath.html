Lesson 7 of 9 · about 20 minutes

🌈 Underneath

The sensors that look down and feel the robot tip. The 3D robot shows the floor's colour, black lines, and being picked up.

🧰 You need

  • The robot and remote, or the pretend robot.
  • Coloured paper, white paper, and black tape.
🤖 Open the remote 🎮 Pretend robot

▶️ Do it

  1. Connect. The round patch under the 3D robot is the floor colour it sees.
  2. Drive onto coloured paper. The patch changes colour.
  3. Drive slowly across black tape. The three rings at the nose go dark.
  4. Pick the robot up. The 3D robot rises.
  5. Tip it each way. The 3D robot leans too.

Pretend robot: drive over the tape and mats; tap 🫳 to pick it up.

🔍 How it works

A third packet, U for under, carries these readings.

  • Line sensors: three lights shining down. Pale floor reflects (low number), black tape soaks it up (high number).
  • Colour sensor: red, green, blue = alvik.get_color(), each 0 to 1. The page paints the patch with them.
  • Picked up: the downward distance reads about 7 cm on a desk. If it grows, the robot was lifted.
  • Tipped: a motion chip gives roll and pitch. The page ignores leans under 8 degrees, and bumps that last only a moment, so it doesn't wobble on a bumpy floor.

New sensor, new packet letter: old pages skip letters they don't know, so nothing breaks.

🧪 Try this

  • Colour hunt. What colour does it think the carpet is? A wooden table?
  • Table edge. Hold the robot with its nose over the edge (don't drive!). What do the rings show?
  • Lift or cliff? When else would the downward distance grow?

✏️ Peek with code

Run this in Arduino Lab for MicroPython and slide paper under the robot:

from arduino_alvik import ArduinoAlvik
from time import sleep_ms

alvik = ArduinoAlvik()
alvik.begin()

while True:
    print("lines", alvik.get_line_sensors(),
          "colour", alvik.get_color(),
          "floor", alvik.get_distance_bottom())
    sleep_ms(300)

The thresholds in sensing.js are partly guesses: tune them with what your robot prints.

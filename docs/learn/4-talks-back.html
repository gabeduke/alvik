Lesson 4 of 9 · about 20 minutes

📡 The robot talks back

Now the robot sends news back, and a 3D robot on the screen copies the real one.

🧰 You need

  • The robot and remote from lesson 2, or the pretend robot.
🤖 Open the remote 🎮 Pretend robot

▶️ Do it

  1. Point the robot away from you, then connect.
  2. Turn it on the spot. The 3D robot turns too, wheels and all.
  3. Press 💡 Lights and 🪩 Disco. The 3D robot copies them.
  4. If the two get out of line, point the robot away from you and tap 🧭.

🔍 How it works

On connect, the page asks ! T 1: "tell me what you are doing". Then about eight times a second the robot sends a ten-byte telemetry packet:

21start !54letter T7B 00heading17distance24lights41speed00turn03switches90checksum
BytesMeansHere
7B 00Heading, tenths of a degree, left = up12.3° left
17Distance ahead, cm (255 = nothing)23 cm
24Lights: a bit each for R, G, B, left then rightboth blue
41Speed, fifths of a cm/s13 cm/s
00Turning, 3°/s stepsnot turning
03Turbo, bumper, blocked, dancingturbo, bumper

Heading comes from odometry: counting how far each wheel has turned. No compass.

🧪 Try this

  • Fool it. Lift the robot and turn the stick: wheels spin in the air. What does the 3D robot do? (Pretend robot: tap 🫳.)
  • Fool it again. Hold it still in the air and turn round with it. Does the 3D robot notice?
  • Drive a square back to the start. Are the two still lined up?

✏️ Change it

On the robot, TELEMETRY_MS = 100 sets how often it sends news. Try 500 and watch the 3D robot move in steps.

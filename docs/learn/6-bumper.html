🛡️ The bumper

The robot stops before it hits a wall. The hard part: not stopping for the floor, a shiny table, or a blip.

🧰 You need

  • The robot and remote (or the pretend robot), and a wall or a big box.
🤖 Open the remote 🎮 Pretend robot

▶️ Do it

  1. Check 🛡️ Bumper says "on". Drive straight at a wall and keep pushing.
  2. At 14 cm it slows down: the lights blink red and yellow heat shows on the screen.
  3. At 8 cm it stops: the lights flash fast and the heat turns red.
  4. Pull back. Reversing always works.
  5. Drive at a chair leg. The real robot bumps into it. Why? Read on.

🔍 How it works

The sensor looks a little down, so on an empty floor it sees the floor about 17 cm ahead. So the bumper only cares about things closer than 14 cm. Then two rules:

  1. Not a blip. A zone has to see the thing 5 readings in a row (about 0.15 s).
  2. Two out of three. Of the middle three zones, two have to see it. One zone's stray reflection does nothing.
seen = [zone.see(d, moved) for zone, d in zip(self.zones, distances[1:4])]
near = sorted(d for d in seen if d is not None)
return near[1] if len(near) >= 2 else None

One more: if the robot drives 4 cm at a "thing" and it comes no closer, it was the floor (the robot is tipped forward a bit), and that zone ignores it from then on.

Closest, or two out of three?

All three at 10: a wall, both rules agree. Only the middle at 6: a chair leg, or a reflection. "Closest" stops; "two out of three" drives on. We picked smooth driving over catching chair legs. That is a trade-off.

🧪 Try this

  • Measure it. Let the bumper stop the robot at a wall. Ruler from nose to wall: is it 8 cm?
  • Dots against heat. On open floor the dots show the floor at 17 cm, and the heat shows nothing. That gap is this lesson.

✏️ Change it

In remote_control.py, SLOW_CM = 14 and BUMPER_CM = 8 set where it slows and stops, and SURE = 5 how sure it must be. Keep SLOW_CM under where your floor shows, or it will slow down for the floor. Lesson 3 says how to change a file on the robot.

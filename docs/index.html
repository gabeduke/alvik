👋 A remote control for the Arduino Alvik robot

Drive an Alvik from a phone, tablet or laptop over Bluetooth. A live 3D robot on the screen copies the real one and shows what its sensors see: walls ahead, the colour of the floor, a black line, being picked up. Free, nothing to install, made by a family for their kids.

📚LessonsHow it works, one step at a time, with things to try. 🔧Put it on your AlvikOver USB from Chrome or Edge on a computer. About 2 minutes.

Bluetooth needs Chrome or Edge (Android, Mac, Windows, Chromebook); on iPad or iPhone use the free Bluefy app. The pretend robot works in any browser. The code is on GitHub. Not made by or connected with Arduino.
